      The routine

      Same five steps on every job

      1. Day 0 · walkthrough

        Hand the card

        Give the review card with the closeout paperwork and say the line. Then move on. Don’t wait while they write.

      2. Day 0 · evening

        Send the text

        Text the link, or email it if they don’t text. Log it here so the reminder lands on time.

      3. Day 4

        One reminder

        No review yet? Send the reminder once and mark it reminded.

      4. Day 10

        Close it out

        Still nothing? Mark it closed. Never ask a third time.

      5. Review posts

        Reply on Google

        Mark it reviewed and reply within 2 business days, good or bad.

      Who does what: the crew lead hands the card and says the line at the walkthrough. Whoever closes out the job sends the text, logs it, sends the reminder and answers reviews.

      Scripts

      Word for word

      Neutral on purpose. Never ask for five stars and never suggest what to write.

      At the walkthrough

      Say it
      Thanks again for having us out. We’re a family-owned company, and most of our work comes from neighbors and referrals. This card has a link to our Google page. If you get a minute this week, we’d really appreciate an honest review.

      Hand the card, then move on to the paperwork.

      Rules
      
      What keeps the reviews safe
      
      
      Breaking these can get reviews deleted or the listing suspended, and some carry FTC penalties.
      
    
      
    
      
      
      
        
      Do
      
        
        
          
      • Ask every customer after every finished job. Asking everyone is what keeps it fair.Google
        • 
          
      • Use neutral words: “honest review,” “share your experience.”
        • 
          
      • Keep it to one ask in person, one text or email, one reminder.
        • 
          
      • Reply to every review, good or bad.
        • 
        
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Don’t
      
        
        
          
      • Offer anything for a review: discounts, gift cards, raffles, free service.Google · FTC
        • 
          
      • Ask only the customers who seemed happy, or check how they feel before sending the link.Google
        • 
          
      • Ask for five stars, or tell customers what to write or which crew member to name.Google
        • 
          
      • Let employees, family, or friends of the company post reviews.Google · FTC
        • 
          
      • Set review quotas or per-review bonuses for the crew.Google
        • 
          
      • Pressure anyone, or stand there while they write.Google
        • 
          
      • Offer anything to change or remove a negative review.Google
